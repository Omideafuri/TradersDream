## 1. STATEMENT OF SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY & USAGE SCOPE

The applicant formally certifies and agrees to the following terms regarding the requested historical market data from Tardis.dev:

1. **Non-Commercial Academic & Research Use (Clause 9.1 & 9.5)**:
   - The data will be used exclusively for internal, non-commercial quantitative market research, backtesting, market microstructure simulation, and statistical validation.
   - The data will not be used in connection with live trading, commercial execution gateways, proprietary capital deployment, or client fund management.
   - The data will not be used to train, fine-tune, or benchmark general-purpose foundation AI models or large language models.
2. **Strict Non-Redistribution of Raw Data (Clause 9.2)**:
   - Raw CSV or NDJSON data files provided by Tardis.dev will be stored exclusively on a secure local research workstation within access-restricted storage paths.
   - Raw data files will not be re-hosted, mirrored, redistributed, sublicensed, or made publicly accessible in any code repository, website, or cloud bucket.
3. **Publication of Derived Academic Results (Clause 9.2(2))**:
   - Public dissemination will be strictly limited to aggregated Derived Data, summary figures, and statistical tables (e.g., mean fill rates, realized spread curves, adverse selection markout curves, and bootstrap test statistics).
   - No raw event records, messages, or reconstructible order-book states will be published.
   - Full citation and proper attribution will be provided to Tardis.dev in all published research reports.
4. **Data Security & Retention (Clause 8 & 9.4)**:
   - The data will be protected against unauthorized access using industry-standard security practices.
   - Downloaded data will be retained and used strictly in accordance with Tardis.dev perpetual internal research terms.
