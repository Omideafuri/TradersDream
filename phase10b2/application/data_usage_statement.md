## 1. STATEMENT OF SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY & USAGE SCOPE

The applicant formally certifies and agrees to the following terms regarding the requested historical market data from Tardis.dev:

1. **Non-Commercial Academic Use**:
   - The data will be used exclusively for non-commercial academic inquiry, quantitative backtesting, statistical validation, and market microstructure modeling.
   - The data will not be used in connection with live trading, commercial signal services, proprietary trading desks, or external fund management.
2. **Strict Non-Redistribution of Raw Data**:
   - Raw CSV or NDJSON data files provided by Tardis.dev will be stored exclusively on a secure local research workstation.
   - Raw data files will not be re-hosted, mirrored, redistributed, sublicensed, or made publicly accessible in any code repository, website, or cloud bucket.
3. **Publication of Derived Academic Results**:
   - Public dissemination will be strictly limited to derived aggregated metrics (e.g. mean fill rates, realized spread curves, adverse selection markout charts, and statistical test outputs).
   - Full citation and proper attribution will be given to Tardis.dev in all published research reports and documentation.
4. **Data Security & Retention**:
   - The data will be protected against unauthorized access.
   - Upon conclusion of the research study, raw data archives will be managed in compliance with Tardis.dev licensing terms.
