## 1. STATEMENT OF SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY & COMPLIANCE

The applicant agrees to the following covenants regarding requested historical market data from Tardis.dev:

1. **Permitted Use (Clauses 9.1 & 9.5)**:
   - Data will be used strictly for internal, non-commercial academic inquiry, market microstructure simulation, backtesting, and statistical validation.
   - Data will NOT be used for live automated trading, proprietary capital deployment, execution routing, or commercial fund management.
   - In compliance with Clause 9.5, data will NOT be used to pre-train, fine-tune, or benchmark general-purpose AI foundation models or LLMs.
2. **Strict Non-Redistribution of Raw Data (Clause 9.2)**:
   - Raw tick CSV files provided by Tardis.dev will reside exclusively on a secure local workstation in protected storage paths.
   - Raw data will never be redistributed, mirrored, uploaded to public repositories, or shared with third parties.
3. **Publication of Derived Academic Results (Clause 9.2(2))**:
   - Public academic outputs will be strictly limited to aggregated Derived Data, statistical tables, and charts (e.g., adverse selection markout curves, queue fill probabilities, bootstrap distributions).
   - No raw event records or reconstructible tick data will be disclosed.
   - Formal attribution and citation will be prominently given to Tardis.dev in all academic outputs.
4. **Data Security & Retention (Clause 8 & 9.4)**:
   - Appropriate technological and operational safeguards will prevent unauthorized access.
   - Historical data downloaded during authorized access will be managed according to Tardis.dev perpetual internal research terms.
