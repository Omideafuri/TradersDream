## 1. PRE-SUBMISSION VERIFICATION CHECKLIST

Before submitting an external data access request or application to Tardis.dev, the user must verify the following:

- [ ] **Accredited Institutional Affiliation**:
  Confirm that the applicant is associated with an accredited university or non-profit academic research institution.
- [ ] **Academic Email Address**:
  Confirm availability of an institutional email address (`.edu` or institutional equivalent) as required by Tardis eligibility verification.
- [ ] **Required Placeholders Populated**:
  Verify that all `[USER INPUT REQUIRED]` placeholders in `phase10b3/application/` have been completed with authentic researcher details:
  - `final_research_project_description.md`
  - `final_data_usage_statement.md`
- [ ] **Data Scope Verified**:
  Confirm that the requested data is strictly limited to `BTCUSDT`, `ETHUSDT`, `SOLUSDT` (`trades` and `incremental_book_L2`) for 30 consecutive calendar days on Binance.US.
- [ ] **Licensing Covenants Accepted**:
  Confirm acceptance of Tardis Terms of Service (Clauses 9.1, 9.2, 9.5) forbidding raw data redistribution and restricting outputs to aggregated derived statistics.
- [ ] **Zero Financial Commitment**:
  Confirm that no funds have been committed and no payment methods have been entered.
- [ ] **Explicit User Submission Authorization**:
  Confirm explicit user intent to proceed with external submission.

## 2. SUBMISSION METHODOLOGY (UPON USER APPROVAL)

If the user authorizes submission:
1. **Contact Channel**: Reach out via official Tardis contact (`https://tardis.dev/#contact` or `support@tardis.dev`).
2. **Application Attachments**: Transmit the finalized project description, dataset specification, and compliance statement.
3. **External Submission Boundary**: **NO AUTOMATED SUBMISSION IS PERMITTED BY THE AI ASSISTANT**. The user must perform or explicitly authorize the transmission.
