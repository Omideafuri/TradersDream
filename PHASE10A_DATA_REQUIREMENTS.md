## 1. DATA SUFFICIENCY DECISION

```text
========================================================================================
                      DATA SUFFICIENCY AUDIT VERDICT:
                 DATA INSUFFICIENT FOR REALISTIC QUEUE MODELING
========================================================================================
```

### Justification
The existing repository datasets consist exclusively of **5-minute aggregated OHLCV bars** (Phases 1–8: 1,500 bars; Phase 9B/9C/9D: 26,000 bars from Binance.US). 
While 5-minute bars are sufficient for macro regime detection and directional bar-level modeling, they are **mathematically and physically insufficient** to simulate passive execution, queue dynamics, partial fills, or adverse selection.

Specifically:
1. **Intra-bar Queue Position is Unobservable**: A 5-minute bar provides only open, high, low, close, and total volume. It reveals nothing about whether our order joined the queue at the front, middle, or back, nor how many limit orders were cancelled or added ahead of us.
2. **Adverse Selection Occurs at Sub-Second Scales**: Microstructure research proves that toxic order-flow adverse selection manifests within 100 milliseconds to 5 seconds of execution. Averaging over 300 seconds (5 minutes) conflates contemporaneous market drift with structural adverse selection.
3. **Fill Realism Cannot Be Tested**: A trade occurring at the bid price within a 5-minute interval does not imply our passive order was filled; in price-time priority, thousands of coins may trade without depleting the queue ahead of our order.
4. **Cancellations and Modifications are Missing**: Without incremental L2/L3 order-book updates, book depletion rates cannot be distinguished from cancellations.

**Conclusion**: Proceeding to simulate queue dynamics on 5-minute data would constitute fraudulent oversimplification. Realistic passive execution research requires tick-level L2 depth updates and normalized trade streams.

## 3. REQUIRED DATA SPECIFICATION (MINIMUM ADMISSIBLE STANDARD)

To transition the data status from `DATA INSUFFICIENT` to `DATA SUFFICIENT`, an independent data acquisition initiative must ingest and validate the following feeds:

### 3.1 Feed A: Incremental Level-2 (L2) Order-Book Delta Stream
* **Sampling Resolution**: Event-driven (sub-millisecond or 100 ms periodic snapshots).
* **Required Fields**:
  - `exchange_timestamp`: Microsecond/millisecond UNIX timestamp generated by exchange matching engine.
  - `local_receive_timestamp`: Local network socket ingestion timestamp.
  - `sequence_id` / `update_id`: Monotonically increasing counter to verify no dropped packets.
  - `side`: Bid or Ask.
  - `price_level`: Price float (exact tick precision).
  - `size`: New aggregated quantity at this price level (0 indicates level depletion/removal).
  - `depth_rank`: Top 10 to Top 20 levels minimum (best bid/ask through L10/L20).

### 3.2 Feed B: Tick-by-Tick Public Trade Stream
* **Sampling Resolution**: Every executed transaction.
* **Required Fields**:
  - `trade_id`: Unique exchange trade identifier.
  - `exchange_timestamp`: Microsecond/millisecond UNIX matching engine timestamp.
  - `price`: Executed price.
  - `size`: Executed quantity.
  - `aggressor_side`: Buyer-initiated (Taker Buy) vs Seller-initiated (Taker Sell).
  - `is_liquidation`: Flag indicating liquidation engine order if supported.

### 3.3 Feed C: Periodic Full Order-Book Snapshots (Book Resynchronization)
* **Sampling Resolution**: Full depth snapshot every 1 to 5 minutes to detect and reconcile packet drops or sequence gaps.
* **Depth**: Top 100 bids and asks.

## 4. TIMESTAMP ARCHITECTURE & LATENCY REQUIREMENTS

Every recorded market event must contain distinct timing fields to prevent lookahead and latency bias:

```json
{
  "exchange_event_time_utc": 1790030100123456,
  "local_receive_time_utc":  1790030100153456,
  "simulated_decision_time": 1790030100155000,
  "simulated_wire_latency":  25000,
  "simulated_arrival_time":  1790030100180000
}
```

* **Network Wire Latency**: Must be explicitly modeled as a stochastic distribution ($\mu = 25\text{ ms}, \sigma = 5\text{ ms}$, Pareto tail up to $150\text{ ms}$).
* **Matching Engine Ingestion Queue**: Must account for order queue serialization at the exchange matching engine.

---

## 5. DATA QUALITY & INTEGRITY CHECKS

Prior to any research usage, the event dataset must pass automated validation:
1. **Sequence Continuity**: Zero gap in `sequence_id` without explicit snapshot recovery.
2. **Crossed Book Prohibition**: Best Bid < Best Ask at all valid timestamps ($t_{\text{spread}} > 0$).
3. **Clock Monotonicity**: $t_{\text{exchange}, i+1} \ge t_{\text{exchange}, i}$ within each single feed.
4. **Volume Conservation**: Aggregated trade volume over any 5-minute interval must reconcile with official exchange 5-minute OHLCV volume within $\pm 0.1\%$.
